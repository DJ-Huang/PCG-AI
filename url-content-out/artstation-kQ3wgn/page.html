    This SOP node divides polygons into panels by iteratively subdividing polygons into smaller 'lots', with control over minimum size, number of iterations, and irregularity.

    The Labs Lot Subdivision SOP has many applications. Within this demo scene, it was used to generate city blocks and building lots, create brick patterns, designate the placement of details like plants, and more!
