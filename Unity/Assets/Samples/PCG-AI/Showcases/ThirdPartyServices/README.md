# Third-Party Service Sample

This sample demonstrates a Meshy-backed image-to-3D graph and the resulting Unity preview. It is isolated here because its inputs and generated model have different provenance from PCG-AI source code.

Before a public release, the maintainer must confirm that `T_meshy_cup_reference.png` and `meshy-cup.glb` may be redistributed under the applicable account and service terms. If that cannot be confirmed, remove those two assets and keep `meshy-image-to-3d.pcg` as an unpopulated integration example.
